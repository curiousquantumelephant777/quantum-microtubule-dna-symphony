## 💡Ideas for Future Experiments Validating or Falsifying Speculative Theories

### 1. Examining Microtubule Photon Dynamics
We could use photomultiplier tubes to measure UPE emission in microtubules, DNA, and mitochondria using brain organoids. If the trace amine superradiance hypothesis is true, introducing exogenous trace amines should result in a synchronized burst of UPE.

### **Experimental Overview & Protocol:**

- **Model:** PMT/EMCCD detection focused on intact human brain organoids or purified tubulin assemblies within a light-tight chamber.
- **Control:** Introduce volatile anesthetics (e.g., isoflurane) to observe the predicted suppression or phase-shift in baseline biophotonic emission (500–550 nm).
- **Variable:** Introduce planarly rigid aromatic trace amines/halogenated phenethylamines/isoquinilones (thought to couple/constructively interfere with phenyl/tryptophan residues in microtubules).
- **Metric:** Measure whether directional halogen bonding within the tubulin tryptophan/aromatic network triggers a phase-locked superradiant photon burst, followed by a subradiant phase correlating with noise suppression. We should also compare this to colchicine- or taxol-exposed microtubules to see if disrupting or stabilizing the  lattice structure affects these dynamics.
