## 2. Core Biophysical Mechanisms

### A. The Terahertz Overlap Domain (0.5 – 2.5 THz)
Biological macromolecules exhibit collective vibrational modes within the far-infrared and sub-terahertz bands. This framework targets the precise window where two cellular systems resonate:
*   **Tubulin Dynamics (0.1 – 1.0 THz):** Microtubule protofilaments generate polar, low-frequency collective vibrations during polymerization and chromosome segregation.
*   **DNA Piezoelectric Resonance (0.5 – 2.5 THz):** Double-stranded DNA exhibits native piezoelectricity; physical strain directly modulates its internal electrical polarization and low-frequency "breathing" modes (radial eigenstates).

### B. Biophoton Phase-Locking & Geometric Resonance
Healthy neural structures function as chiral metamaterial networks. The right-handed helical configuration of mitotic spindles generates a coherent, circularly polarized phononic eigenstate profile that is phase-locked with the matching right-handed geometry (and potential UPE waveguide emission) of the DNA helix. Malformed or transformed cells present with distorted spindle geometry, breaking this topological phase-lock and radiating chaotic electromagnetic noise.

### C. The Isoquinoline Mechanical Switch
The proposed molecular motif utilizes an asymmetric, highly polarizable rigid isoquinoline core intercalated into the DNA base pairs:
1.  **Phase-Locked Window:** Coherent UPE drives the molecule’s electron cloud into a stable, non-reactive exciton state. The molecule remains passive. Iboga-type alkaloids are thought to induce a global low-energy phase-lock between these two systems, analogous to the quantum Zeno effect or BootROM, which freezes the system in place/blocks a wide array of receptors (compressed spring) which allows for a snap-back to juvenile/coherent conformation of neurons.
2.  **Phase Disruption:** Incoherent THz noise from a malformed cell triggers a rapid, asymmetric Photo-Induced Electron Transfer (PET), introducing stochioastic noise that the cell may react to by upregulating survival-related or primitive transcription factors, which may play a role in disease and oncogenesis.
3.  **Phononic Transformation:** Because the molecule is non-centrosymmetric, the sudden electronic shift instantly alters its atomic spacing. This structural "snap" converts the photon energy directly into a localized **acoustic phonon shockwave** (a solitary wave or soliton) traveling along the DNA backbone. This may induce a piezoelectric/biophotoelectric effect where the coupled phonon-biophoton interactions drive biological phenomena (e.g., kinesin and dynein propagation, DNA transcription, synchronized synaptic wave propagation), especially within the linker of nucleus and cytoskeleton (LINC) complex.

## 5. Falsifiability
*   **Thermal Decoherence:** *In vivo* biological systems are warm and wet ($37^\circ\text{C}$), which typically forces quantum coherence to decay into random thermal motion within femtoseconds. This model posits the quantum phenomena originate from phonon-biophoton spin-spin coupling within the LINC complex and synchronized, quantized phononic eigenstates (e.g., synchronized brain wave propagation).
*   **Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR):** Native biophotonic emissions are exceptionally weak. Differentiating a true phase-lock failure from background metabolic noise requires an extreme molecular sensitivity threshold.
